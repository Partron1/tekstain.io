# Design Style Prompt — Tekstain Nexus ESG Platform

Act as a senior UI/UX designer and design-system specialist. Define and apply a consistent visual style for the Tekstain Nexus ESG and Carbon Management Platform, designed for SMEs and larger organizations in Ghana.

First, review the existing application and available project documents, especially `PRD.md`, `Frontend_PRD.md`, `Architecture.md`, and the project knowledge base. Treat existing requirements and decisions as authoritative. Do not invent product features or change business logic.

Create a clean, credible, modern sustainability-focused design style that communicates trust, clarity, environmental responsibility, and data accuracy. Specify and consistently apply:

- **Color system:** primary, secondary, accent, neutral, background, border, success, warning, and error colors, with accessible contrast.
- **Typography:** readable font choices, a clear type scale, weights, line heights, and hierarchy.
- **Layout:** spacing scale, grid, content widths, responsive breakpoints, and page structure.
- **Components:** consistent buttons, form fields, cards, tables, navigation, tabs, dialogs, badges, charts, and status indicators.
- **Data visualization:** clear labels, units, legends, reporting periods, and accessible color use; never make incomplete data look like zero or imply unsupported ESG performance scores.
- **Interaction states:** hover, focus, active, disabled, loading, empty, success, and error states.
- **Accessibility and responsiveness:** keyboard-friendly interactions, visible focus, readable text, semantic structure, and usability on mobile, tablet, and desktop.

Use visual elements purposefully. Avoid excessive decoration, cluttered dashboards, misleading environmental imagery, inconsistent colors, low-contrast text, and unnecessary animation. Ensure carbon and ESG metrics remain easy to interpret and their units, reporting periods, and relevant limitations are visible.

If the project already has a design system, improve and extend it rather than replacing it without a clear reason. Keep design tokens reusable and component styles consistent. Make only design-related changes unless explicitly authorized to change functionality.

**Deliverable:** document the design tokens and component rules, then apply the agreed style consistently across the existing interface. Summarize the files changed and any accessibility checks performed. Do not claim checks passed unless they were actually run.
